### 6. Manual Portal Submission Steps (For Students)
1. Navigate to your college VEDIC portal login page (e.g., `https://vedic.edu.in` or college intranet URL).
2. Enter your institutional credentials (Student Roll Number & Password).
3. Navigate to **Academics** > **Project Submissions** > **Final Year / SDG Projects (25 Marks)**.
4. Select Project Title: **Digital Learning Management System (DLMS)**.
5. Select SDG Theme: **SDG 4 — Quality Education**.
6. Paste your public GitHub repository URL.
7. Paste the Firebase Hosting live URL.
8. Upload `PROJECT_REPORT.pdf` (export from `docs/PROJECT_REPORT.md`).
9. Upload the zipped screenshot package.
10. Click **Submit for Internal Guide Review** and save your submission confirmation receipt number.
