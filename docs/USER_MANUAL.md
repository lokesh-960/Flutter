# USER MANUAL & OPERATION GUIDE
# Digital Learning Management System (DLMS)

## 1. Introduction
This manual provides end-to-end instructions for using the **Digital Learning Management System (DLMS)** across all three role workspaces:
- **Part A: Student Learner Guide**
- **Part B: Course Instructor Guide**
- **Part C: Platform Administrator Guide**
- **Part D: Evaluator Quick-Testing Guide**

---

## Part A: Student Learner Guide

### 1. Registration & Authentication
1. Launch the application. On the Splash/Onboarding screen, click **Get Started** or **Skip**.
2. To sign up, select **Create Account**:
   - Provide your Full Name, Academic Email, and a secure Password (at least 6 characters).
   - Ensure the **Student Learner** account type chip is highlighted.
   - Click **Create My Account**.
3. To log in with existing credentials, enter your Email and Password on the Sign In screen and tap **Sign In**.

### 2. Discovering & Enrolling in Courses
1. From the Student Dashboard, tap **Browse Catalog** or select the **Courses** tab.
2. Use the **Search Bar** to find topics by keyword (e.g., `"Python"`, `"Database"`).
3. Filter by **Category** (Programming Fundamentals, Java, Web Development, etc.) or **Difficulty** (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced).
4. Tap any course card to open the **Course Details** view.
5. Review the syllabus, learning objectives, and prerequisites.
6. Click **Enroll Now (Free Access)**. A confirmation banner will indicate successful enrollment.

### 3. Studying Lessons & Tracking Progress
1. Open any enrolled course and tap a curriculum module to expand lessons.
2. Tap a lesson to open the **Lesson Workspace**:
   - Read the structured notes, explanations, and formatted code blocks.
   - Note the estimated reading duration badge.
3. Upon finishing the lesson, click **Mark Complete**. Your progress bar will immediately increment.
4. Use **Next Lesson** or **Previous Lesson** buttons to navigate sequentially.

### 4. Taking Certification Quizzes
1. When all lessons in a course are completed, navigate to the course overview or quiz card.
2. Tap **Take Quiz**:
   - A live countdown timer shows remaining time in the top-right corner.
   - Select multiple-choice answers for Question 1 through N.
   - Tap **Next Question** to proceed.
3. On the final question, tap **Submit Assessment** and confirm submission in the dialog.
4. The **Results Screen** will display your score, percentage, pass/fail status, and a detailed question-by-question review with pedagogical explanations.

### 5. Viewing Badges & Profile
1. Tap the **Profile** icon in the app bar to view your details.
2. Select **Milestone Achievements** to see unlocked badges (`First Step`, `Knowledge Seeker`, `Certified Scholar`, `SDG 4 Champion`).

## Part B: Course Instructor Guide

### 1. Accessing the Academic Studio
1. Log in with an Instructor account (or switch to the Instructor role via the Profile screen).
2. The **Instructor Studio** displays authored courses, active published curricula, and total enrolled students.

### 2. Creating a New Course
1. Tap **Create Course** or the floating `+` action button.
2. Complete the course form:
   - Course Title, Description, Subject Category, Difficulty level, and Total Duration in minutes.
3. Tap **Create Course** to commit the curriculum.

### 3. Authoring Modules & Lessons
1. Open the course from the Instructor Studio by tapping the **Edit (Pencil)** icon.
2. Scroll to the **Curriculum Modules** section and tap **Add Module**:
   - Enter the Module Title (e.g., `Module 1: Syntax Basics`) and description.
3. In the created module, tap **Add Lesson**:
   - Provide Lesson Title, Estimated Reading Minutes, and Markdown/Code content.
   - Tap **Save Lesson**.

### 4. Publishing & Visibility Control
- In the Instructor Studio, toggle the **Eye Icon** next to any course to instantly switch between **Published** (visible in catalog) and **Draft** (hidden from students).

## Part C: Platform Administrator Guide

### 1. Accessing the Governance Console
- Log in with Administrator credentials (`admin@dlms.edu` / `Password123!`).

### 2. Managing User Roles & Permissions
1. From the Admin Console, tap **Manage User Roles**.
2. Search any registered user by name or email.
3. Tap the **Edit (Pencil)** icon next to the user.
4. Select the new permission tier (**Student**, **Instructor**, or **Administrator**) and tap **Update Role**. The permission takes effect immediately.

### 3. Moderating Course Content
- Under the **Curriculum Moderation** section on the Admin Dashboard, use the toggle switches to take down or unpublish any course that fails institutional standards.

### 4. Broadcasting Campus Announcements
1. Tap **Post Announcement**.
2. Enter the Announcement Headline, detailed message, and Priority (`Normal` or `Urgent`).
3. Tap **Broadcast**. The announcement will appear at the top of all student dashboards.

## Part D: Evaluator Quick-Testing Guide

To rapidly evaluate all features during a viva or presentation:
1. **One-Tap Demo Credentials**: On the Login screen, click any of the 3 chips under "Evaluator Quick-Fill Credentials":
   - Tap **Student** -> fills `student@dlms.edu`
   - Tap **Instructor** -> fills `instructor@dlms.edu`
   - Tap **Admin** -> fills `admin@dlms.edu`
2. **Instant Role Switcher**: Navigate to the **Profile** screen (`/profile`) and use the **Evaluator Role Switcher** chips to toggle between Student, Instructor, and Admin without logging out.
